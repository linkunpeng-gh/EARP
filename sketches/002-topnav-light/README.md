## Variant: Topnav Light

### Design stance
Modern SaaS platform — Stripe-like white surfaces, top navigation, card-driven layout. Clean, bright, professional.

### Key choices
- Layout: sticky top header (56px) + centered max-width (1200px) content
- Color: white/gray palette (#fafbfc root, #ffffff panels, indigo accent)
- Typography: Inter 600 weight headings, 500 weight navigation, subtle shadows
- Interaction: quick-action cards hover → accent border + shadow; secondary button with border

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: professional, readable, familiar SaaS pattern, good for light-mode users
- Weak at: less distinctive than dark theme, may feel "generic" without strong branding

### Best for
Enterprise admin users who work during daytime. Bright environments where dark mode causes glare.
